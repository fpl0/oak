# About Oak

Oak teaches music theory by having you write music in [Strudel](https://strudel.cc), a live-coding language for patterns. It runs entirely on this computer: your progress lives in a local database, sounds are served from a local pack, and nothing leaves the machine unless you turn the tutor on.

## Sounds

The music you make with Oak's sounds is yours. Most of the sounds are public domain (CC0). The Rhodes (jRhodes3d by Jeff Learman) and the Salamander Grand Piano (Alexander Holm) are CC BY, credited below as their authors ask. Oak renders its own drum machines, breaks and wavetables and releases them CC0. Four instruments (acgtr, clav, pad, choir) and the `gm_` sounds still come from older General MIDI soundfonts whose licences Oak could not verify; they will be replaced. The full list, with links and licences, is under **Sound credits** below and in the pack's `CREDITS.md`.
