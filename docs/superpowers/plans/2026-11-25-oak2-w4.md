## Review Focus

The five ways W4 is likeliest to fail the learner in real use, each pinned by a test in its owning task:

1. **CF-A's ending line refuses real endings, or passes a piece that just stops.** A required rule that misreads a fade written with a signal, an outro where parts leave over the last section, or a final hit after an `arrange()` would block the core's last release; a loop that simply stops, or a "cadence" that is the loop's own last bar, would pass the book's point by surface. Pins: CF-A brief tests (Task 1 Step 3: "just stops" and "the cadence heard earlier" fail with `rubric.unmet`; exit-order outro, final hit and fade pass; every `CF_A_PIECES` passing piece still passes), `content/validate/oak2-capstones.test.ts` "ends-on-purpose is required on CF-A only" (Task 1), `bun run content:check CF-A`.
2. **The F2 nudge nags or lies.** It fires after a real structural change, fires with no passing draft to compare, compares against a draft that never passed, always plays the earlier draft as "1", or comes back after every tweak once dismissed. Pins: X18 AC table (Task 3: notes, method names and string contents are changes, not tweaks; swing-back needs a version older than the one before), `nudge.test.ts` (Task 2: "no passing check, no nudge", "a structural edit since the passing check, no nudge", "the earlier side is 1 for some seeds and 2 for others", "dismissed stays dismissed until a newer passing check"), `check-history.test.ts` "a refused check writes no row" (Task 2).
3. **Matched loudness or normalisation damages what the learner hears or keeps.** The quieter side turned up into clipping, a silent take raised by +∞, a take normalised twice, a decode failure that loses the take, or a peak above −1 dBFS on the album. Pins: X19 AC 4–7 (Task 4: silence gives gain 1, the peak cap holds, input never mutated), `matched.test.ts` "never turns a side up" (Task 2), `normalize.test.ts` "a decode failure keeps the original take", "a silent take is refused before decoding", "the WAV reads back at the target within 0.5 LU with its peak at or under −1 dBFS" (Task 2).
4. **The Shelf hides or loses work.** Shelving un-releases a capstone or changes its gates, a shelved track disappears from everywhere (not even the Shelf), a sketch shelved through two calls is left on the album when the second fails, or a shelved sketch's lines stop being scraps. Pins: `shelf-w4.test.ts` (Task 1: "a capstone released to the Shelf stays released and its side still counts it", "album.get lists a shelved track under shelf only, never under tracks", "a B-side created shelved never appears in tracks", "patch back puts it on the album", "the sketch's lines are still listed by scraps.list", "backup round-trip keeps shelved").
5. **The honest review breaks old reviews or ignores the question.** A stored 1.4 review without `answer` fails to parse on the album or the Brief; zero strengths crash or show an empty "Strengths" heading; the question is printed but answered last or not at all; learner text in the question escapes its line. Pins: X20 AC 1, 3, 5, 9, 10 (Task 5), `review-card-w4.test.tsx` "a stored review without answer renders as before", "zero strengths: no strengths list", "the answer comes first" (Task 2).

import type { AnalysisSummary } from "@oak/api"; // or @oak/content-kit, whichever exports it (read)
export type EditKind = "tweaks" | "swing-back" | "changes";
export interface EditVersion { version: number | null; code: string; analysis: AnalysisSummary | null }

Behaviour (binding for the brief):
1. Pure; no Strudel, no DOM. Input in any order: versions with a number are sorted by `version`; a `version: null` entry (the live code) is last.
2. Fewer than 2 entries → `"changes"`.
3. **Swing-back** (checked first): let `last`, `prev` be the last two. `normCode(last) !== normCode(prev)` and `normCode(last)` equals `normCode` of some entry before `prev` → `"swing-back"`. 4. **Tweaks**: every consecutive pair has equal `codeShape` → `"tweaks"` (a pair whose `normCode` is equal counts as a tweak: nothing heard changed).
5. Otherwise `"changes"`.
6. The tokenizer knows `//` and `/* */` comments, `"…"`, `'…'` and `` `…` `` strings (escapes included; a template's `${…}` is part of the string), and numeric literals `12`, `1.5`, `.5`, `1e3`, `0x1f`; a leading `-` is a separate token, so `-1` → `-2` is a tweak. Anything inside a string (mini-notation, note names, degrees) is not a value: changing it is a change.
7. `analysisKey` is a stable JSON (sorted keys) of `{ bars, form, roles, slots: <"bar:beat:symbol">[], cadences: <"bar:type">[], devices: sorted }`; `energy`, `bpm`, `durationSec`, `key`, `keyContexts`, `deviceList` and `schemas` are left out (a tempo or level tweak must not read as a new piece).
