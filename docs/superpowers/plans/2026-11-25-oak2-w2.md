## Review Focus

The five likeliest ways a W2 lesson or song change misleads or fails the learner, each pinned by a test in its owning task:

1. **A piece keeps the surface and misses the book's point, and passes.** Worst cases known now: `hocket` passing whole bars traded between two sounds (call and response, not a hocket); `rotated-copy` passing a time-shifted copy when the motif's rhythm is even; `carved` passing an additive build; `ends-on-purpose` passing a crash on a bar heard before; `stepped-control` passing a per-note sweep. Pinned by each owner's book-miss pins (Tasks 1–4, 8–10) and the runner's coverage test (Task 7 Part A Step 3: every graded W2 step has a book-miss and a book-other-way pin).
2. **A right answer written another way fails.** A hand-written same-shape chord loop (no `.transpose`), an inversion around another centre, a kick moved into the bass's gaps instead of the bass moved, a cadence on a new last bar as an ending, a background part that steps forward by rhythm rather than gain. Pinned by the book-other-way pins and the "three other right answers" tests (Tasks 1–4, 8–10).
3. **A song or example change breaks a lesson.** A take's reference stops fitting (clash, flavour roots, `start-passes`), a question about an example becomes false (A2.L06 S3's answer is "eight bass notes"; X4 §3), or a lesson's `startFrom` loop already meets the new required rule. Pinned by `content:check` of every lesson of the unit + `unit-songs.test.ts` after every song edit (Tasks 1–5), and by the lessons' own investigate tests, which are never edited for a song fix.
4. **A locked tool or a later word reaches a lesson at its earliest gate.** `.early` in U07.L11, mode names in J3.L05, `.late` or `marimba` in A2.L07–L09, "verse" in a U08 `today` line. Pinned by `content:check <id>` checks 4 and 14 and by one lint pin per lesson in its pins file (Task 1 Step 4, Tasks 8–10 AC: the "obvious shortcut" with the locked tool fails).
5. **Sound pack v5 changes a v4 sound.** A rebuilt kit or alias with new bytes or a new level shifts every song, Band and loudness budget at once. Pinned by `scripts/sounds/v5-lock.test.ts` (Task 6 Step 1: every v4 lock entry's sha256 is unchanged in v5) and X13's level ACs (± 1 dB of the role's v4 level).

## The music-fix protocol (binds Tasks 1–5)

For every song, Band or example a task owns:

1. **Read the three verdicts.** The audit row (`audit-core.md` / `audit-rest.md`), X4's row where X4 sampled it (`audit-crosscheck.md` §1–§2: U02, U05, U09, U13, J3, A2 and the pop, jazz-soul and electronic Band styles), and X4's general corrections (§2 "Factual and implementation corrections"): a once-only event *inside* a section heard once counts (PLAN §5 says "inside a section, ≤ 1 bar"; it does not require the section type to repeat); continuing parts across a boundary are a seam; existing velocity shapes are looseness; overlap is not proven masking.
2. **Classify each proposed fix:**
   - **Apply** — the checklist item is *no* in both readings (or *no* in the audit and unsampled by X4), the fix is structural (a varied repeat, a once-only bar, a seam, an exit-order ending, a peak moved), it lives in a part that survives the takes or in Oak's opening listen, **and** you re-read the evaluated song and confirm the gap is real under X4's corrections (write the bar numbers you found).
   - **Listen first** — the evidence is register overlap, kick/bass overlap counts, a drum nuance (alternate-hand hats, ghosts, feathered kick, `.late` pushes), a sound or filter choice, or a Band-wide feel change. Not made in W2. Write the exact proposed code and the bars in your hand-back; the integrator puts it in the "Listen" list of `docs/decisions.md`.
   - **Drop** — X4's evidence-backed verdict is *yes*, or the fix breaks a rule or a lesson (the four X4 §3 conflicts below). Record "dropped: X4 §… " in your hand-back.
3. **Keep the song rules:** section ids, types and bars unchanged; tune pitches, bass downbeat roots (silence included) and chord roots identical across the five flavours (`usong.flavour-*`); the §13 clash rule; one builder; new material only as variant consts (`hatsChorus2`) through the builder's `ByKind.only`; `UnitSongDef.version` +1 per changed song.
4. **Verify each applied fix on the rendered result and on its lessons, never on event overlap alone:** evaluate the song headlessly (a scratch script calling `strudel(code, bars)` from `packages/grader/src/analyze/__support__/fixtures.ts` in the pop and electronic flavours at least) and read the events of the changed bars; `bun run content:check <id>` for **every lesson of the unit**; `bun test --isolate ./content/validate/unit-songs.test.ts ./content/unit-songs/index.test.ts`; the loudness harness for the unit (targets must hold). A fix that models a W2 rule is also checked with that rule (U09's outro with `ends-on-purpose { modes: ["exit-order"] }`).
5. **Hand back a Listen line for every applied fix too** ("U09 outro: Oak's chords leave after bar 62, bass after 63, kick and crash alone on 64"), PLAN §7.
