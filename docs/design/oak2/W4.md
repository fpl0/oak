- `@oak/audio/loudness`: `loudness`, `peakDb`, `loudnessOf`, `peakDbOf`, `normalizeGain` (never raises what it cannot measure; a peak cap), `applyGain` (new arrays). `packages/strudel/src/audibility/quality.ts` re-exports `loudness` and `peakDb` from it (one implementation); the import boundary allows `strudel → audio` (`types/import-boundaries.test.ts`, `docs/design/interfaces.md` §2).

- Stricter than before: `loudnessOf`, `peakDbOf` and `normalizeGain` throw `RangeError` on non-finite samples or mismatched channel lengths (the moved `loudness`/`peakDb` too). `normalizeRecording` treats any throw as "keep the original take".

## 6. Known gaps (W4 leaves these open)

1. **W3's sibling and chain player is not level-matched.** `BackToBack` plays live through a hidden Workbench, and its `gainOf` seam needs each item's level; the honest way is to render every item offline and measure it (what `MatchedCompare` does for two), which is seconds per item before Play and a change to W3's player. Not done; siblings play at their written levels.
2. **A transcription's pickup and clock are not carried into a borrowed form** (decision 128): a song with a pickup gets its sections from bar 1.
3. **Album WAV exports are rendered, not normalised**; only release takes are (decision 125).
4. **Whether the Workbench's mute chips follow the erase pass's mutes** is unverified: the pass sets mutes through `EditorHandle.setMutes`, and the browser spec reads `getMutes()`, not the chips.
5. **The fake tutor's answer is one fixed sentence** (`FAKE_REVIEW_ANSWER`); the real `claude` CLI's handling of the nullable `answer` type under `--json-schema`, and the live API with the new required key, are unverified (cassettes only).
6. **Studio's loop length after Fill it all** is not updated: a 4-bar sketch filled to 16 bars may still show its old length (`SketchEditor` owns `readLength`).
7. **`staggered-boundary`, `unique-event` and `arc` read "needs sections"** on an unsectioned draft of C3, C4, C7 or JC3; they are suggestions, so nothing is blocked.
8. **A shelved capstone release** shows a toast ("On the Shelf: finished, and kept off the album."), not a line in the release ceremony (`Ceremony.tsx` was not W4's).
9. **The matched compare's "now" side** uses the newest saved version's Band; a Band changed but not yet saved is not heard there.
10. **A gain-only tweak that changes a version's stored analysis** (for example a part dropping under the audibility floor) reads as a change or a swing-back, not a tweak; the unit tests use `analysis: null`. On the safe side: no nudge.
11. **Browser-only parts unverified by unit tests**: decoding a real MediaRecorder take (`decodeAudioData`) and how long decode + measure + encode takes on a long take; whether the compares sound matched; real audio of the CF-A outros, the final hit and the fade. Nobody has listened. The wave gate's browser specs (`w4-shelf`, `w4-form-start`, `w4-nudge`, `w4-erase`, `w4-review-question`, `studio.pw.ts`) cover the main flows; see the integration report for which ran.
12. **The browser-pane walk of PLAN §11** (finish and shelve, borrow a form, release to the Shelf, the nudge, the erase pass, a review with a question, a normalised take in Settings › Data) was not done at the W4 gate.
