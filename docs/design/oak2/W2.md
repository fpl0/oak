## 2. Rules as the W2 lessons use them

The 17 rules are W0's (`docs/design/oak2/W0.md`); the adversarial review (X14) found book-miss pieces that passed and right answers that failed, and W2 changed these (decisions 105–110). Every threshold is Oak's call and says so in `packages/grader/src/rubric/oak2-*.ts`.

| Rule | As used after W2 |
|---|---|
| `ends-on-purpose` exit-order | ≥ 2 parts stop for good inside the last section, **in at least two different bars**, none before a part that came in after it. Two parts may stop together as one stage (decision 106). |
| `ends-on-purpose` shrinking-tail | Unchanged: reads the whole texture, so a shrinking tune over a one-bar bass riff fails (decision 108, listen first). |
| `carved` | Each bar of a section is a sub-multiset of the fullest section's bar **at the same place** (looped when longer); a moved or reordered bar is new material. `minDiffer` counts versions of the peak: a line-up of parts, or every part kept with **at most 75 % of the fullest's onsets per bar** (`CARVED_THIN`, decision 107). |
| `unique-event` | A track's bar heard once inside a repeated section, **or a one-bar stop**: the track silent for that bar, playing around it and at that place of every other time the section comes. |
| `arc` | A section counts as thinner at **exactly** a quarter fewer onsets per bar (`THINNER` 0.25, read as `contrast` reads it). |
| `same-shape` | Heights are **pitch classes** of the lowest note: an octave is no move. Also used by U03.L10 and U04.L04 (both still pass). |
| `bass-names-chord` | Each stack counts **for as long as it sounds** over its bass; `min` (default 0.75). |
| `gap-fill` | A leap is filled when the next note turns back **and lands inside the leap's span** (not on or past its start). |
| `stepped-control` | For `cutoff`, `hcutoff` and `bandf` a jump between held values is **at least a third of an octave**; other controls keep "at least a fifth of the range". |
| `kick-bass-composite` | `minBassPerBar` holds **in every full bar**, not on average. |
| `matches` (freeze) | U07.L10 S5 and U09.L04 S9: `min` 0.82 with `wPitch` .7 / `wRhythm` .3, i.e. 3 in 4 pitches with the rhythm kept; an octave move counts half (decision 109). |

Unchanged and pinned as built: `staggered-boundary` (but see §5, its period reading), `inverted-copy`, `rotated-copy`, `time-shifted`, `stack-intervals`, `linear-drums` (it refuses an evenly spaced backbeat, so R1.L06's examples keep kick and snare uneven), `grouping-accents`, `depth-layers`, `chords-per-bar`, `no-chance`, `hocket` (not used on this branch).
