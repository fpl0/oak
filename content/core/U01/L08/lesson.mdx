<Step id="S1">
Every groove you have written, from Heartbeat to your four-bar phrase, counted in fours. This one does not.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<Callout kind="tip">The band leans on 1 with its bass and answers on 2 and 3 with its chords: oom, pah, pah.</Callout>

<OnPass>Four does not fit. The kick comes back after every three beats, so each bar here holds three beats instead of four.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Now put the count in your hands.

<Tap exercise="tap-on-one" />

<OnPass>Your taps marked the strong beat of every bar. That lilt, strong-weak-weak, is a waltz.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
The drums line has three words, and the tempo line ends in a different number.

<OnPass>The number after `/` is the beats in a bar, and the words in the quotes share that bar. How many beats a bar holds, and how they are grouped, is the **meter**. This groove is in three.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
One more bar size, for contrast: two beats, `setcpm(100/2)`. Marching bands walk to it.

<OnPass>Left, right: two steps to a bar, where the waltz went strong-weak-weak. On paper each bar size is two numbers stacked at the start of a piece: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4. The top number counts the beats in a bar, and the bottom 4 says each beat is a quarter note. That pair is the **time signature**, and its top number is the one after `/` in the tempo line.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Here the tempo line and the drums line disagree.

<Editor exercise="make-it-a-waltz" />

<OnPass>Kick, snare, snare in a bar of three: a waltz at 108. Both halves matter: `/3` makes the bar three beats long, and three words give each beat one sound.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Now by ear alone. Find the kick, then count to the next one.

<EarDrill family="fam.rhy.meter" level={1} trials={6} />

<OnPass>You can now tell three from four with no code to read: a question you can ask of any song you hear.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
Now a waltz of your own. Keep the kick on 1 and the groove sways with the bass. Brackets from Two per Beat work in three as well: hats in pairs split each of the three beats in two.

<Make exercise="your-waltz" />

<CanDo id="cd.hear.meter" />

<Recap />
</Step>
