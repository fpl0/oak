<Step id="S1">
Count to Four left you a kick on 1 and 3. Here it plays on a drum machine with two more lines, first all together, then with one line taken out.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>The second groove lost its snare. The kick still lands, but nothing answers it, and the band sounds strangely lost. Next you find out where the snare sits.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Each drum now has a line of its own. The `~` you learned last time keeps every line four beats long.

<Predict exercise="where-the-snare-lands" />
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Here is the same groove written another way, in one line with `stack()`. Oak keeps one line per drum instead, so each part has a name you can mute, read and change. <CodeRef fn="stack" />

<Example id="stacked" views="code" />

<OnPass>The snare. It plays on 2 and 4, the beats a crowd claps on.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
You found the snare by clapping. Now write it in.

<Editor exercise="add-the-snare" />

<OnPass>Kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4: the **backbeat**. It sits under a huge share of the pop, rock, funk and hip-hop you have heard.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Two drums can share one line, as they did in Heartbeat. Here, give each its own.

<Editor exercise="one-line-per-drum" />

<OnPass>Same sound, clearer code: every drum on its own labelled line.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Now put the backbeat in your hands.

<Tap exercise="tap-the-backbeat" />

<OnPass>Once your hands know where the snare goes, you will start hearing it in song after song.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
`.bank()` swaps the whole kit for another drum machine. `RolandTR808` and `RolandTR909` play Oak's own versions of the 808 and the 909, two of the most recorded drum machines ever made. <CodeRef fn="bank" />

<OnPass>The rhythm is in the words; the kit is in `.bank()`. Change one and the other stays put.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S8">
In Heartbeat a beat was one line of four words. Now it is a kit, one line per drum, each with its own job. Your kit plays the second part of your song, the eight bars where the tune comes in: move the kick, add an open hat `oh`, mark the 1 with a crash, try another machine.

<Make exercise="your-backbeat" />

<CanDo id="cd.compose.backbeat" />

<Recap />
</Step>
