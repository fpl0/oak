<Step id="S1">
Here is a groove with a kick on an and, played on a new kit: the LinnDrum. You hear it three ways, with the band at one steady tempo throughout.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>The `.slow(2)` version feels heavier: the drums stretch out while your nod and the band stay put. The `.fast(2)` version crams twice as much into the same time and feels urgent. The tempo never changed. Only the drums' pace against it did.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
`.slow(2)` stretches a pattern over twice as many bars. It goes into the chain straight after `s("…")`. <CodeRef fn="slow" />

<OnPass>Same tempo, same band, but the snare now lands on beat 3, once a bar instead of twice. That is **half-time**. Drummers switch to it to make a section feel heavier without slowing the tempo. `.fast(2)` does the opposite and squeezes the pattern into half a bar: **double-time**.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Copying grooves by ear is how many drummers learn. One drum at a time is easiest: snare first, then kick.

<Callout kind="tip">Count 1-&-2-&-3-&-4-& while it plays. Every hit lands on one of those eight words.</Callout>

<OnPass>Kick on 1, 3 and the & of 4, snare on 2 and 4: copied by ear. That last kick pushes into the next bar.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Turn the hook into its half-time twin.

<Editor exercise="make-it-heavier" />

<OnPass>Three lines, three `.slow(2)`. The whole groove stretches together, so it keeps its shape at half the pace. Slow one line alone and it still lines up with the others, but the parts fit together in a new way. You can try that in your own groove at the end of this lesson.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
One more way a groove can change its feel, with no new hits and no new tempo. B's hats use a tool you have not met yet, so for now, only listen.

<Compare a="hook" b="rolling" />

<OnPass>B. Its hat between two beats arrives late, so each beat splits long-short instead of into two equal halves. You will write that feel yourself later in the course.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
At the start of this unit a groove was four words on one line. Now you can split its beats, give it a fill, copy one by ear and flip it to half-time. Your backbeat is in the editor once more; at its new speed it plays the quiet part of your song, before the last loud part. Try a new kit while you are at it, `AkaiLinn` or `RolandTR707`, with some colour on top: a shaker `sh`, a cowbell `cb`, a tambourine `tb`.
