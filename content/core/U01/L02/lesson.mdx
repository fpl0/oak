<Step id="S1">
Heartbeat gave you four even beats to a bar. Counting them tells you which beat is which.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>The crash: it comes once a bar and sends your count back to one. The three kicks after it carry the count on to four.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Now your hands mark where the count starts, and your head counts the beats in between.

<Tap exercise="tap-the-one" />

<OnPass>Beat 1, where the count starts again, is the **downbeat**. Drummers mark it with a crash, bass players with their first note, and listeners with a slightly deeper nod.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
The `~` has been in your loop since Hello, Loop. Here it sits where a kick used to be. <CodeRef fn="~" />

<Predict exercise="which-beat-goes-quiet" />
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Watch the roll while it loops: three kicks, and a gap the size of one beat.

<Example id="gap-run" views="code,roll" />
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
One careful edit. The bar shares its length among however many words it holds, so count them before you run.

<Editor exercise="silence-beat-3" />

<OnPass>That `~` is a **rest**: a beat with no sound that still takes its place in the bar. The count goes on through it, which is why the snare on beat 4 still lands on 4.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Drummers rarely hit every beat equally hard. `.gain()` sets loudness from 0 (silent) to 1 (full). <CodeRef fn="gain" />

<OnPass>A louder downbeat helps a band stay together: everyone can hear where 1 is.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
Many pop and rock grooves stand on a kick that plays every other beat.

<Editor exercise="kick-one-and-three" />

<OnPass>`bd ~ bd ~`: two kicks and two rests, every beat accounted for. Beats 2 and 4 are open now, and the next lesson puts a snare in them.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S8">
In Heartbeat every beat needed a sound; now you choose which beats stay silent. Your beat from Heartbeat is in the editor, and it becomes your song's stop: the band hits once on beat 1 and leaves the rest of the bar to your drums. A crash or a louder `.gain()` can mark the 1, and any drum you have works: `bd`, `sd`, `hh`, `oh`, `cr`, `rd`, `cp`, `rim`.
