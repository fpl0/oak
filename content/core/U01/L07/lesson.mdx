<Step id="S1">
Every groove you have written so far loops after one bar. Songs are built from longer stretches: here is a groove that comes round every four bars.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>One bar in every four breaks away from the backbeat, and the bar 1 after it lands like an arrival. The groove now has a shape longer than a bar.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Until now a pattern in quotes played whole in every bar. Angle brackets change that.

<Predict exercise="one-per-bar" />
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
`<bd sd>` gave one item to each bar: `bd` in bar 1, `sd` in bar 2, then round again. The hook's snare line uses the same trick, with a whole bar in `[ ]` for each item.

<Callout kind="tip">To hear a groove without one line, write `_` in front of its label, as in `_snare:`. The line stays in your code but goes quiet. Remove the `_` to bring it back.</Callout>

<OnPass>Bars 1 to 3 keep the backbeat. Bar 4 breaks it with snares and toms and runs into the next bar 1. That break is a **fill**: it tells the listener a phrase is ending and the next one is about to start.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Now you decide where the fill goes.

<Parsons exercise="end-on-the-fill" />

<OnPass>The groove bars can come in any order. The fill only works last, right before bar 1 comes round again.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
A one-bar loop goes round and round without ever arriving anywhere. Give it a shape.

<Editor exercise="close-the-phrase" />

<OnPass>Three bars of backbeat and a fill: a four-bar phrase. `[~ sd ~ sd]!3` inside the `< >` writes the three repeated bars once.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Two bars this time, heard as one pair.

<Tap exercise="echo-two-bars" />
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
Now your grooves can last four bars and end on a fill. You start from your one-bar backbeat, and your phrase takes over the second part of your song: it plays twice there, so its second fill comes just before the stop. Any line can change: a kick on an and from Two per Beat, hats that drop out, a crash on bar 1, a tom run written with `!`.

<Make exercise="four-bar-phrase" />

<CanDo id="cd.compose.fill" />

<Recap />
</Step>
