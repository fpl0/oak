<Step id="S1">
In Lock with the Kick the bass took the kick's rhythm, so the two hit as one. There is an opposite way to join them: let the bass play only where the kick is silent. Here are both, over the same chords.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>In loop 1 each bass note lands on a kick and the two blur into one thud. In loop 2 they take turns: kick, bass, kick, bass, bass. You hear every hit, and together they make one bouncing line down low.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Taking turns is about where a note stops as well as where it starts. A bass note that is still sounding when the kick hits runs into it.

<Choice exercise="short-or-held" />

<OnPass>Card B: each bass note is gone before the next kick lands, so the kick hits a clear space. In card A the held note smears across beat 3.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Here the bass plays an eighth note on every beat, under a kick on 1 and 3.

<Example id="count-the-collisions" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Two: on beats 1 and 3 the bass and the kick start together, and on beats 2 and 4 the bass is alone. Those two collisions are what you move next.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
The kick stays put. Only the bass's rhythm changes.

<Editor exercise="bass-into-the-gaps" />

<OnPass>The kick and the bass now take turns, and the low end reads as one rhythm handed back and forth. Two parts heard as one line like this make a **composite line**. Funk and house bass players write this way all the time.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
The start of every note is already in a gap. Its end is not.

<Editor exercise="stop-before-the-kick" />

<OnPass>`.clip(.5)` cuts every bass note to half its written length, so the held note stops on beat 3 as the kick hits. Where a note ends matters as much as where it starts.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Your kick and bass play the first loud part of your song, over your chords from Root–Five–Octave and Oak's tune. The last two loud parts keep the lock you wrote last lesson, so the song has both ways of joining kick and bass. You can move the bass into the kick's gaps, move the kick into the bass's, and shorten notes with `.clip()`, or let a long note ring past a kick as long as it starts in a gap.

<Make exercise="kick-and-bass-take-turns" />

<CanDo id="cd.compose.kick-bass-line" />

<Recap />

You can now lock a bass to the kick or set it against the kick, and hear which one a song wants. Next: the sound of your bass.
</Step>
