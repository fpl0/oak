<Step id="S1">
Before Strudel places chords for you, one more round by hand: the last check of Voice Leading by Hand, moved to G. Hints 1 to 3 are fine, and you can try as often as you like.

<Editor exercise="hand-voiced-cadence-again" />

<OnPass>G holds into C, every voice moves a short way into D, and D holds into the last G. Your hand path works in any key.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Until now you wrote every note of every chord. Here the chords are written by their names, the chord symbols from Chord Names, and Strudel works out the notes.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>They glide: the top note goes C, B, C, C, and nobody wrote a single note. Strudel kept every chord's top near the same height, much as your hand path did.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Strudel placed those notes for you. Did it place them the way you would?

<Choice exercise="yours-or-strudels" />

<OnPass>Card B is Strudel's: four or five notes a chord where yours had three, with some letters doubled. The details differ, but both glide, the idea you followed by hand.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
One chord name, and the notes Strudel picked for it.

<Example id="one-c" views="code,staff" />

<OnPass>Only C, E and G, the three letters of a C triad, whatever octave each one sits in. Strudel may double a letter or put the 3rd at the bottom; the chord is still C.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Your hand path from the last lesson, written out note by note. Say the same thing in four names.

<Editor exercise="say-it-by-name" />

<OnPass>Four names instead of twelve notes, and Strudel chose where each note goes. Which octave each note of a chord sits in, and which one is on top, is the chord's **voicing**. `.voicing()` picks one for every chord and keeps them near the same height, so each moves smoothly to the next.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
You still decide where the chords live. Move them up and listen to the colour change.

<Editor exercise="lift-them" />

<OnPass>Same chords, same smooth motion, placed higher. The anchor moves the whole voicing; the names stay the same.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
Last lesson every note of every chord came from your hand. Now four names do that work, and you still choose their sound and, with `.anchor()`, their height. They play the last loud part of your song, under Oak's tune, so they keep its chords, C G Am F. A bass of roots, low on the bass clef, gives them a floor.

<Make exercise="loop-by-name" />

</Step>
