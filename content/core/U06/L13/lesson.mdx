<Step id="S1">
Every chord you have stacked so far came from the key: the same stack of degrees, moved with `.add()`, turned major on 1 and minor on 6. This loop does something else, and you will hear it at once.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>One colour, four heights. The same dark chord, a minor chord with one more 3rd on top, slides from C to F, Eb and Bb, and never changes its mood. It is the sound of deep house, and it leaves C major on purpose.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
The same four chords can be played two ways: each note stepping to the nearest note of the next chord, as you did with inversions, or the whole stack moved at once.

<Choice exercise="close-or-as-a-block" />

<OnPass>Card B. When the whole stack moves, every note moves by the same distance, so the chords slide rather than step. Card A sounds smoother and more settled; card B sounds like one chord on the move.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
The hook does all of its moving with one function, `.transpose()`, which you met when you moved notes by half steps.

<Example id="how-far" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Three half steps, and all four notes moved: C Eb G Bb became Eb Gb Bb Db. `.transpose()` moves every note of the line the same distance, so the gaps inside the chord stay the same.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
One shape, held for four bars. Make it slide to the target's four heights.

<Editor exercise="slide-the-shape" />

<OnPass>C, Bb, Eb, F: four chords, one shape. A chord shape moved as a block, every note by the same number of half steps, makes **parallel chords**. Because the shape never adapts to the key, the chords bring in notes from outside it, and that is the colour.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Now by ear, with three-note chords this time.

<Choice exercise="which-one-slides" />

<OnPass>Card A: C minor, A minor, F minor, G minor, one colour on every root. Card B played the key's own chords, C, Am, F and G, so its colour changed with every bar.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Take the shape, or build your own, and slide it. Mix in what you have: `.struct()` for the rhythm of the hits, a bass on each chord's lowest note. Until today the key chose your chords; now you can choose one colour and move it anywhere.

</Step>
