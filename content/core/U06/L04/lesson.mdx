<Step id="S1">
In Melody Workshop a tune paused in bar 4 and came home in bar 8. Chords can do the same, and now you can name them.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>One half takes a breath and waits; the other settles, with the tune on 1. Together they sound like a question and its answer.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
A sentence pauses at a comma and stops at a full stop. Listen to where each half of the hook stops.

<Choice exercise="which-one-is-finished" />

<OnPass>Bars 5–8 end V to I and sound complete. Bars 1–4 stop on V, the tension chord, and wait for the answer. The chords that end a phrase are its **cadence**. A phrase that stops on V ends with a **half cadence**: a comma, not a full stop.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
V to I can close with more or less weight. The tune on top makes the difference.

<Example id="two-endings" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Both endings go V to I, the closed ending: an **authentic cadence**. With the tune on 1 over chords with their roots at the bottom, it is perfect, the most final ending there is. With the tune on 3 or 5 it is imperfect: closed, but softer.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Now by ear alone, and home moves from key to key.

<EarDrill family="fam.har.cadence" level={1} trials={6} />

<OnPass>Closed or open, in any key: the same two endings, wherever home is.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
A longer loop now, with an ending to mark at each half.

<OnPass>Phrase 1 stops on V: a half cadence. Phrase 2 goes V to I with the tune on 1: a perfect authentic cadence. A question, then its answer.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Now turn a question into an answer.

<Editor exercise="close-the-phrase" />

<OnPass>`<0 3 4 0>`: I, IV, V, I. Bar 3 builds the tension and bar 4 releases it: home, away, tension, home in four bars.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
In Melody Workshop the tune alone asked and answered; now the chords can do it, and you can name both endings. The starter asks its question twice and never answers. A tune that ends on 1 over the last I makes the ending perfect.

</Step>
