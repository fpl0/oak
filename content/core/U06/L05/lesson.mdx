<Step id="S1">
In a full loop your ear goes to the tune and the top of the chords. The line at the bottom is easier to follow than it seems, and it tells you more.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>In each bar the bass drives on one note, the root of its chord: C, G, A, F. Only beat 4 leaves it, on the way to the next bar.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Now put those roots in your own voice.

<Sing exercise="sing-the-roots" />

<OnPass>Do, sol, la, fa: degrees 1 5 6 4, the roots of I V vi IV. When the bass sits on the roots, singing it gives you the numerals.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Now loops you have not heard. Listen past the chords to the bottom note, and find each one from home.

<EarDrill family="fam.har.bass" level={1} trials={4} />

<Callout kind="tip">Sing the bass note quietly before you press a degree. Your voice finds do faster than your ear alone.</Callout>

<OnPass>Bass first: four notes, and the loop is half named.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
A loop with its code hidden. Write only the bass, then switch between the source and yours at the same bar until they match.

<Editor exercise="take-down-the-bass" />

<Callout kind="tip">The bass is judged by its degrees and its rhythm, in any octave. If it hides under the chords, slow it down.</Callout>

<OnPass>Do, la, fa, sol: `0 5 3 4`, degrees 1 6 4 5. Under triads on their roots that is I vi IV V: C, Am, F, G. A loop you had only heard is now on paper, bass first.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
The loop you took down, on the grand staff: the chords above, your bass line below, its degrees under each note.

<Staff example="on-the-page" />
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
The chords are a line of your code this time, and the bass is yours; in your song they play the eight bars after the first loud part, with Oak's tune on top. A note of the chord on each beat 1 keeps the loop steady. The root is the safe one; the 3rd puts the chord in first inversion, as in Inversions. Between those notes the line can walk, repeat, or lead into the next bar like the hook's beat 4.

</Step>
