<Step id="S1">
Every minor tune in this unit so far was one you wrote or one Oak made up. Minor is also home to many old songs. First a ballad setting with two sounds new to you, strings and an echo, on Am, F and E; then a spiritual over the same chords.

<Example id="hook" views="code" autoplay />

<OnPass>Each piano note came back softer a moment later, and the strings held on underneath: the echo and the long chords give a slow minor tune room to breathe. Go Down Moses comes home to A, and its last climb into home runs through G♯, the raised 7 of Raise the Seventh.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
The tune's code and roll stay hidden; yours show as you write. Listen for home first, then the first note, then where the tune moves.

<Editor exercise="take-it-down" />

<OnPass>Sol below home, then up to me me re re, me me do: a minor tune you took down by ear, home on A with no chord needed to tell you so.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
The key signature is empty, as in C major: A minor shares its notes. The one sharp is written on its note, G♯, the raised 7, ti. The chord names sit over the bars where they start.

<Sing exercise="sing-from-the-page" />

<OnPass>Read and sung: a line that falls to home, and an answer that drops to sol and climbs through ti back to do.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
No code and no roll this time: only the page.

<Editor exercise="play-from-the-page" />

<OnPass>Sol sol ti ti do: the answer climbs through the raised 7 and lands home, the strongest arrival a minor key has. You have both halves of the line now.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
The strings, the upright and soft drums are set; the tune still sits on home. A ballad needs space: where the tune holds a long note, there is room for a reply on another part, as in Call & Response.

<Make exercise="a-minor-ballad" />

<CanDo id="cd.transcribe.minor-tune" />

Nine lessons ago minor was one word in `.scale()`. Now you can take a minor tune down by ear, read it from the page and set it as a ballad. Next: five notes with no wrong ones, then the Minor checkpoint, which opens your minor indie ballad.

</Step>
