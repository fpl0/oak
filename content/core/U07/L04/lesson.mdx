<Step id="S1">
A tune can fit its chords and still sound stiff. First comes one made only of chord tones on beats 1 and 3, like the landings of the last lesson; then the same tune with its gaps filled.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>They join it up. The plain tune jumps from note to note; the filled one walks. Each added note sits on a weak beat and moves by step, so you hear motion, not a mistake.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
A plain move from C to E skips a degree. Here one note fills the gap.

<Predict exercise="on-the-way" />
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Back to the filled-in tune, now with the chord-tone lens open.

<Example id="outside-the-chord" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Four notes, one in every bar, are not in their chord, and every one of them is a step from the notes on both sides. A tune note that is not in the chord under it is a **non-chord tone**. It rubs for a moment, then moves to a note that is. D in bar 1 and F in bar 3 walk from one chord tone to the next, in one direction: each is a **passing tone**.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
A new tune over the same two chords. Its notes on beats 1 and 3 stay where they are; the gaps on beat 2 are yours.

<Editor exercise="connect-the-notes" />

<OnPass>F, B and G pass through, from one chord tone to the next. In bar 3, G on both sides: the gap steps off it and comes straight back. A non-chord tone that steps away from a chord tone and returns to it is a **neighbour tone**, above it (A) or below it (F).</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Six short tunes now, each with one note marked on the staff.

<EarDrill family="fam.mel.nct" level={1} trials={6} />

<OnPass>Through or around: the note after the marked one decides. Same direction, it passed; back where it came from, it was a neighbour.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
On the staff a passing tone fills the gap of a 3rd: line, space, line, or space, line, space. A neighbour tone steps to the next line or space and comes straight back.

<Staff example="on-the-page" />
</Step>
