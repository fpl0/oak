<Step id="S1">
A tune you may already know, in 3, with a pickup like the ones you just wrote. This time the music is on a page: notes on the staff, chord names above them.

<LeadSheet example="hook" />

<OnPass>The tune moves in long-short steps: a long note for two beats, then the third beat for one note or two quick ones. The page holds all of it, and the names above the staff say which chord sounds under each bar.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Everything a player needs is on one page. Read it the way a band player would: the tune along the staff, the chords from the names above.

<LeadSheet example="read-the-page" />

<OnPass>B♭ over bar 3, and a pickup of one beat. A song on one page, the tune on a staff with the chord symbols above it, is a **lead sheet**. It is how jazz standards and many pop songs are passed between players: one page, and everyone plays their own part from it.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Play from the page: no code for the tune and no roll, only the lead sheet. The long-short steps you heard are written as half notes and quarter notes, with two eighths sharing a beat in bar 1.

<LeadSheet example="the-page" />

<Editor exercise="play-from-the-page" />

<OnPass>A rest, a rest, then C on the last beat of bar 0; then F held for two beats and A F in one. You read the pickup, the half notes and the eighths straight off the page.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Now sing it from the same page, the way a singer reads a part they have never heard.

<Sing exercise="sing-from-the-page" />

<Callout kind="tip">No microphone, or Quiet switched on: read the page and hear the tune in your head, then play Oak's version and compare.</Callout>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
The page names the chords as well. Write them under the tune.

<LeadSheet example="the-symbols" />

<Editor exercise="chords-from-the-symbols" />

<OnPass>F, F, B♭, F, with nothing under the pickup. The symbols name the chords; how you voice them is yours, stacked by hand or by `.voicing()`. Both read the same off the page.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
A last page, in another key.

<LeadSheet example="one-bar" />

<Choice exercise="read-one-bar" />

<OnPass>D over bar 3, and A on its beat 1: a note of the chord, as the first note of a bar usually is.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
Only the drums play, a waltz in 3. Grow the tune if you like: a pickup into bar 1, or eight bars that ask and answer. You can now read a song off a page, and write a page someone else could play from.

<Make exercise="your-own-page" />

<CanDo id="cd.read.leadsheet" />

<Recap />
</Step>
