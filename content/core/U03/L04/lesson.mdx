<Step id="S1">
Two degrees are still missing from your ears: 6 and 7, the top of the scale. This line climbs through them to home.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>Right at the top, just before the high 1. Bar 2 makes you wait for it: the line turns back once before it lands.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Three notes up from 5, and then nothing.

<Predict exercise="stop-on-seven" hum />
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Writing a climb one number at a time gets long. The opening line's first bar used a shorter way. <CodeRef fn=".." />

<Example id="run" views="code,roll" />
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
A range counts down as well as up. Keep the spaces around `..`: written as `0..7`, Strudel cannot read it as a range, and that part of the bar goes silent.

<Editor exercise="up-and-back-down" />

<OnPass>`[0 .. 7]`, then `[7 .. 0]`: sixteen notes in two words. A range fills the place of one note, so in a bar of its own it takes the whole bar.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
The last two degrees by ear. Listen for which way the note wants to move.

<EarDrill family="fam.deg.fet-major" level={3} trials={6} />

<Callout kind="tip">7 sounds almost home already. If the note seems to strain upward, it is probably 7.</Callout>

<OnPass>All seven degrees by ear. 7 pulls hardest: it sits a half step under home and wants to rise to it. Degree 7 is the **leading tone**, because it leads you home. In C it is the `b4` that left you hanging in Home & the Major Scale.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Now put every pull you have heard into your voice.

<Sing exercise="lead-them-home" />

<OnPass>Three resolutions: 7 up to 1, 6 down to 5, 2 down to 1. Each restless note found the stable one beside it.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
All seven degrees are now yours, by ear and in code. Your line from Restless Neighbours is back, for the four bars just before the first loud part of your song, which begins on the high home, `c5`. A run with `..` gets you to the top fast, and an `n` past 6 lets 7 lead up to the high home.

<Make exercise="let-seven-lead" />

</Step>
