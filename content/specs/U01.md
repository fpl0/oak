Nine lessons that turn 'I can't keep time' into 'I can write, read and copy a drum groove'. Everything is drums: no pitch until U02. Every Make is a groove over the Band's bass and chords (`tracks: ["bass", "chords"]`), so a one-bar beat already sounds like a record. ## Conventions for this unit

- Labels from day one: `drums:` for a whole kit on one line (U01.L01–L02), then one labelled line per drum (`kick:` `snare:` `hats:`) from U01.L03. Never `$:`, never a capital letter (STYLE §2).
- Tempo line `setcpm(bpm/4)` in every code string (`setcpm(bpm/3)` in U01.L08). Keep tempos between 84 and 124 so the Band sounds natural; one tempo per lesson unless the step is about tempo.
- Counting words before their lesson: say 'beat 1', 'beat 3', 'the and of 2' freely from U01.L02; 'downbeat' only from U01.L02's pass text, 'rest' from U01.L02, 'backbeat' from U01.L03, 'subdivision' from U01.L04, note-value names from U01.L05, 'fill' from U01.L07, 'meter' and 'time signature' from U01.L08. content:check fails a term used before its step and advises on later lessons' terms.
- No pitch words (high, low, note names) as teaching content in U01: pitch is U02.L01's idea. 'A higher-sounding drum' is fine as description.
- Panels: roll (drum lanes) only, until U01.L05 adds the percussion staff (`staff: "perc"`); never more than two of roll, staff, keyboard.
- Band for every Make: `band.two-chord-vamp` with `tracks: ["bass", "chords"]` (the learner writes the drums), `band.waltz-vamp` in U01.L08. The Band shares the lesson tempo.
- Tap steps: `tol: { windowMs: "auto", tempo: "locked", forgiveOffset: true, passF1: 0.8, passTiming: 0.7 }`; tests give tap times in seconds from the stimulus start (U01.L01 shows the helper). Tap never gates and is never one of the graded checks.
- Feedback codes the grader really emits for drum steps: `code.unchanged`, `rhythm.missing`, `rhythm.extra`, `rhythm.displaced`, `rhythm.subdivision`, `rhythm.duration`, `code.tempo`, `code.tempo-factor`, `code.labels`, `code.must-use`, `rubric.unmet`. ## U01.L01 Heartbeat

Author: **content-lead**. Key C major, 4/4, 96 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** Feel pulse and beat, set a tempo with `setcpm(bpm/4)`, learn that 1 cycle = 1 bar, write a one-line beat.

**The one idea.** Music has a steady beat; the first line of the code sets how fast it goes, and the code in quotes is one bar.

**Sound first.** A two-line drum groove (kick-snare on the beats, hats twice as fast) over the Band's bass and piano, 96 BPM. The learner nods along before reading anything.

```js
setcpm(96/4)

drums: s("bd sd bd sd")
hats: s("hh*8")
```

**Skills.** Introduces: R01 Pulse & beat; R02 Tempo / BPM (needs R01); R03 Meter 4/4, bar & downbeat (needs R01); E01 Tap & rhythm echo (needs R01). Practises: none.

**Terms (≤ 3, a pair counts once).** `term.pulse` "pulse" in S2: The steady beat you tap your foot to. `term.tempo` "tempo" in S4: How fast the pulse goes, counted in beats per minute (BPM). `term.bar` "bar" in S5: A group of beats; in Oak one cycle is one bar.

**Can-do.** `cd.hear.pulse` "I can find the beat in a groove and keep it going."; `cd.code.tempo` "I can set a tempo and write a one-line beat."

**Unlocked here (content/toolbox.ts).** `tb.s` `tb.setcpm` `tb.mini.seq` `tb.mini.repeat` `snd.drum.bd` `snd.drum.sd` `snd.drum.hh` `snd.drum.cp` `snd.drum.rim`

**Tools the learner's code uses.** `tb.s` `tb.setcpm` `tb.mini.seq` `tb.mini.repeat` `tb.label` `snd.drum.bd` `snd.drum.sd` `snd.drum.hh` `snd.drum.cp` `snd.drum.rim`

**Steps.**

## U01.L02 Count to Four

Author: **U01a**. Key C major, 4/4, 96 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** Find the downbeat, count 1-2-3-4, use rests as rhythm.

**The one idea.** Beat 1 is where the bar starts, and a silent beat still counts.

**Sound first.** A crash on every beat 1 over a steady kick and hats, so 'one' jumps out before it is named.

```js
setcpm(96/4)

drums: s("cr bd bd bd")
hats: s("hh*8")

**Skills.** Introduces: R06 Rests (needs R03). Practises: R03 Meter 4/4, bar & downbeat (needs R01).

**Terms (≤ 3, a pair counts once).** `term.downbeat` "downbeat" in S2: Beat 1 of a bar: where the bar starts and the groove lands. `term.rest` "rest" in S5: A beat that is silent on purpose and still counts.

## U01.L03 Backbeat

Author: **U01a**. Key C major, 4/4, 100 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** Kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4; layer parts as one labelled line per drum (`kick:` `snare:` `hats:`).

**The one idea.** Kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4: the backbeat, written one line per drum.

**Sound first.** A classic backbeat on a TR-808 over the Band; then the snare drops out for two bars and comes back.

kick: s("bd ~ bd ~").bank("RolandTR808")

## U01.L04 Two per Beat

## U01.L05 Seeing Rhythm

## U01.L06 Sixteenths & 1-e-&-a

Author: **U01b**. Key C major, 4/4, 92 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** Four per beat, spoken counting, tom fills.

**The one idea.** Four per beat, counted 1-e-&-a, and the toms that make a fill.

**Sound first.** Sixteenth-note hats over a backbeat, and a quick tom run on beat 4 of every bar.

setcpm(92/4)

hats: s("hh*16").gain(.5)

```

**Skills.** Introduces: R07 Sixteenths & 1-e-&-a (needs R05). Practises: none.

**Terms (≤ 3, a pair counts once).** `term.sixteenth-note` "sixteenth note" in S3: A quarter of a beat: four of them, counted 1-e-&-a, fill one beat.
