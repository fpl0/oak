## A1.L01 Waves & Harmonics

Author: **spec-A1**. Key C major, 4/4, 100 BPM. A side lesson: outside the default order, offered from its gate on, written for a learner at the gate.

**Objective (syllabus).** The harmonic series; see a wave in Oak's scope panel.

**The one idea.** Every pitched note is a stack of sine waves at 1, 2, 3, 4 … times its frequency, the harmonic series, and a wave's colour is which harmonics it has and how loud: a sine only the first, a square and a triangle the odd ones, a sawtooth all of them. `.scope()` shows the shape; `.partials([…])` chooses the harmonics.

**Sound first.** The A1 loop twice within 20 seconds: bass and riff on sines (thin, far away), then the same notes with the bass on a six-harmonic sawtooth and the riff on a square (full, close).

```js
setcpm(100/4)

kick: s("bd ~ bd ~")
snare: s("~ sd ~ sd")
hats: s("hh*8").gain(.5)
bass: note("<[c2 ~ c2 g1] [f1 ~ f1 c2]>").s("sawtooth").partials([1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1])
mel: n("<[4 4 2 4 ~ 2 0 ~] [5 5 3 5 ~ 3 0 ~]>").scale("C4:major").s("square").partials([1, 1, 1, 1, 1]).gain(.8)
```

**Skills.** Introduces: D02 Oscillators & harmonics (needs D01). Practises: none.

**Terms (≤ 3, a pair counts once).** `term.frequency` "frequency" in S4: How many times a wave repeats each second, in hertz (Hz): A4 is 440, the A an octave below 220. `term.harmonic-series` "harmonic series" in S5: The pitches at 1, 2, 3, 4 … times a note's frequency, from the note itself up: which of them a sound has, and how loud, is its colour. A sine has only the first.

**Can-do.** `cd.code.harmonics` "I can see a wave's shape and hear its harmonics."

**Unlocked here (content/toolbox.ts).** `snd.wt` `tb.scope` `tb.partials`

**Tools the learner's code uses.** `tb.setcpm` `tb.label` `tb.mini.seq` `tb.mini.rest` `tb.mini.sub` `tb.mini.alt` `tb.mini.repeat` `tb.gain` `tb.s` `tb.s.pitched` `tb.n` `tb.scale` `scale.major` `tb.note` `tb.note.names` `snd.drum.bd` `snd.drum.sd` `snd.drum.hh` `snd.wave.sine` `snd.wave.triangle` `snd.wave.square` `snd.wave.sawtooth` `tb.scope` `tb.partials` `tb.note.accidentals`

## A1.L02 Filters & Filter Envelopes

Author: **A1**. Key C major, 4/4, 100 BPM. A side lesson: outside the default order, offered from its gate on, written for a learner at the gate.

**Objective (syllabus).** Cutoff, resonance and movement.

**The one idea.** A low-pass filter lets a sound's low harmonics through and takes away those above its cutoff: lower the cutoff and the sound gets darker (`.lpf()`; `.hpf()` takes the lows away instead, `.bpf()` keeps a band). Resonance (`.lpq()`) adds a ringing peak at the cutoff, and a filter envelope (`.lpenv()` with `.lpa() .lpd() .lps() .lpr()`) moves the cutoff on every note: the pluck of a synth riff.
