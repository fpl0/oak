## Conventions for this unit

- Keys and the Band: every U02 lesson is in C major (`key: { tonic: "C", mode: "major" }`) and every Make uses `band.two-chord-vamp` (I–IV: C then F). A melody on white keys always fits it. Do not say 'key', 'scale', 'tonic' or 'degree' in prose: those are U03's words. 'Home' is fine in plain English.
- Note names: lowercase letter, optional `#`/`b` (from U02.L03), octave digit always (`c4`, never `c`; STYLE §3). Melody register octave 4–5; bass notes stay in the Band. In prose use the same spelling as the code (`c4`, `f#4`); in pass text the musical name may follow once (`f#4`, F sharp).
- Before U02.L02 only `c` appears in learner code (U02.L01 teaches octaves with one letter). Before U02.L03 no sharps or flats in any code string the learner edits.
- Panels: roll with `rollLabels: "note"` plus the keyboard until U02.L04, then the treble staff (`staff: "treble"`) plus one of roll or keyboard. Never all three.
- Every notated example (a step with a staff panel) must engrave with `approximate = false` and no warnings: whole-beat and half-beat values, no ties or dots (`@2` and `_` are fine: they make halves and wholes). content:check runs the notation engine on it.
- Repertoire (U02.L08): Ode to Joy (theme) and When the Saints, from content/repertoire. Neither may start with a pickup before U07.L08: When the Saints is set from its first full bar, its 'oh when the' written on beats 2–4 of a bar that opens with a rest; Ode to Joy's dotted bar 4 becomes `e4 d4 d4@2` (no dots before U13.L03). Say 'a plain-rhythm version'.
- Feedback the grader emits for note steps: `code.unchanged`, `melody.octave`, `melody.wrong-degree`, `melody.missing-note`, `melody.extra-note`, `kb.wrong-note`, `kb.octave`, `id.wrong`, `rubric.unmet`. The catalog words `melody.wrong-degree`, `melody.missing-note` and `melody.extra-note` in scale degrees and solfège ('you have 5 (sol)'), which U02 does not teach: every pitched code step overrides those three in `feedbackText` (content:check fails it otherwise), and `melody.octave` too until U02.L01 has named the octave. Copy U02.L01 S5's overrides. Run `bun run content:check <id> --verbose` to read the exact text.

## U02.L01 High & Low

Author: **content-lead**. Key C major, 4/4, 92 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** Pitch and octaves; `note("c3 c4 c5").s("piano")` (the letters are explained in L02); higher/lower by ear.

**The one idea.** Notes sound higher or lower, and the number after the letter moves a note up or down by an octave.

**Sound first.** A piano line climbing through three octaves of C and falling back, over the full Band, 92 BPM.

```js
setcpm(92/4)
```

**Objective (syllabus).** Semitone and tone, sharps and flats, enharmonics.

**The one idea.** Neighbouring keys are a half step apart, two half steps make a whole step, and black keys are sharps or flats.

**Sound first.** A chromatic creep (e4 f4 f#4 g4) answered by whole steps (eb4 f4 g4 a4): tight versus open.

**Skills.** Introduces: P03 Half & whole steps (needs P02); P04 Accidentals & enharmonics (needs P03). Practises: none.
