The last unit of the jazz track (contracts §2.6), where the changes the learner can already play start to sound like the records: the same melody over richer, moving harmony. Four devices, each first heard on a record-like trio and then swapped in by the learner into a progression they already know: the tritone substitution, D♭7 for G7, so the bass slides down by half steps (J5.L01, the exemplar); the backdoor, Fm7 B♭7 into C, home from a whole step below (J5.L02); the secondary ii–V, a ii–V aimed at any chord of the tune (J5.L03, with the passing diminished chord heard once beside it); and all of them put to work under a tune whose melody does not move, Aura Lee's verse as a jazz ballad (J5.L04, the passing diminished chord's own lesson). ## Conventions for this unit

- Written for the learner at the gate (contracts §7.6 rule 5, AUTHORING §20.1): J5 opens after U17.CP AND J4.L04, so the whole core (secondary dominants, borrowed chords, key changes, chromatic `n` steps, the tenor and the horns) and J1–J4 are behind the learner, except J2.L04's chord-scales (it can be passed over): never ask for `locrian`, `altered`, `lydian:dominant` or `bebop` in J5. J5 unlocks no tool: every device is a chord symbol the learner can already write.
- One device a lesson, heard before it is named (AUTHORING §3 rule 1). Each lesson opens with the device on a record-like example (a jazz trio's last line, a ballad on the tenor), names it in the pass text of the step where the learner first writes it, and ends with a Make in which the learner swaps it into a progression they already know: the ii–V–I (J1.L03), the I–VI–ii–V turnaround (J4.L01), a two-five chain, Aura Lee's verse (U16.L05). The devices' words wait for their lessons: "tritone substitution" is J5.L01's, "backdoor" J5.L02's, "secondary ii–V" J5.L03's, "passing diminished chord" J5.L04's (J5.L03 hears it and does not name it), "Coltrane changes" J5.L05's. "Reharmonization" is U16.L05's term and may be used throughout.
- What each device is, as the grader detects it (`detectDevices`, packages/grader/src/analyze/piece.ts; content-kit `JAZZ_DEVICES`), and as every prose sentence must say it. Tritone sub: a dominant seventh whose root is a half step ABOVE the chord it moves to (D♭7 → C; G♭7 → F); it shares the 3rd and 7th of the V7 it replaces (G7 and D♭7 both voice F4 B4 in `guidetones`, evaluated). Backdoor: the minor iv7, then ♭VII7, then I (Fm7 B♭7 Cmaj7 in C; theory `schema.backdoor`). Secondary ii–V: a minor seventh (or m7♭5) followed by a dominant seventh a 4th above it, aimed at a chord other than home (Em7 A7 → Dm7 in C; Bm7♭5 E7 → Am7 for a minor target). Diminished passing: a diminished seventh a half step above one chord and a half step below the next, so the bass climbs by half steps (C, C♯°7, Dm7). Taught in these directions only: the descending passing diminished (Em7 E♭°7 Dm7) and the common-tone diminished (C°7 over C) are not detected and never asked for (probed: both fail `device-count`).
- Code (STYLE §5.1): symbols by name in `chord()`, `C^7 C7 Cm7 Cm7b5 Co7`, a flat root with `b` (`Db7`, `Gb7`, `Bbm7`), a sharp with `#` (`C#o7`, `F#7`). Enharmonic spellings sound the same and pass a `pcset` check (`F#7` for `Gb7`, `Cb7` for `B7`, `Dbo7` for `C#o7`, probed): prose spells a tritone sub from the flat side (D♭7, G♭7) and shows the sharp spelling once where it is easier to read. Two chords in one bar are a bracket (`[G7 Db7]`); a passing chord on the last beat is `[C^7@3 C#o7]` (tb.mini.elongate, U01.L05). `.dict("lefthand")` voices every seventh, the dominants and `Co7` included, but `C6`, `F6`, `Cm9`, `Eb9` and `C7alt` are SILENT in it (evaluated; contracts §6.7 item 6): J5 code keeps to the five sevenths under `lefthand`, and every loop carries a `bass:` line.
- The sound of the unit: J1's trio (the ride pattern and the hi-hat on 2 and 4 on the jazz kit, an upright, Rhodes comping on the Charleston with `.dict("lefthand")`) with the vibes (L01, L03) or the tenor (L04, L05; `snd.tenor`, U16.L05) on top; ballads at 72–76 BPM (L02, L04), medium swing at 120–144 (L01, L03, L05). Swing: `.swingBy(1/3, 4)` on every line with off-beats, declared on the lesson (`swing: { subdivision: 8, amount: 1/3 }`).

## J5.L01 Tritone Substitution

Author: **spec-J**. Key C major, 4/4, 120 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** ♭II7 for V7.

**The one idea.** G7 and D♭7 share their 3rd and their 7th (B and F; in D♭7 the B is spelled C♭), so either one leads into C. Put D♭7 where G7 was and the bass slides down by half steps, D, D♭, C, under the same two guide tones: the tritone substitution, ♭II7 for V7.

**Sound first.** The last four bars of a jazz ballad by the trio, twice: Dm7 G7 Cmaj7, then Dm7 D♭7 Cmaj7. The vibes play the same line both times; only the bass of bar 2 moves, from a fall of a 5th to a slide of a half step.

## JC4 Reharmonize a Standard
