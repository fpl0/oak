<Step id="S1">
Your rootless voicings comp under a tune. An arranger often goes further and puts a chord under every note of it. Here the Rhodes harmonises a four-note vibes tune from below, over Dm7 G7 Cmaj7, in two takes; only the Rhodes changes.

<Example id="drop-2-trio" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>The first take is thick and a little boxed in: four notes crowd right under the tune. In the second, one note of each chord drops lower, the sound opens up, and the tune still sits clearly on top.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Find the note that opened the sound: one chord, two ways.

<Example id="one-move" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Bar 1 is Cmaj7 in **close position**: C E G B, all four notes inside one octave. In bar 2 the G, second from the top, went down twelve half steps, an octave, and the chord opened to G C E B. That is a **drop-2 voicing**: the same chord, the top note unchanged, the inside spread out.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Now with your hands, one chord at a time.

<OnPass>A3 D4 F4 C5: Dm7 opened, with C still on top. Three notes kept their place and one went down an octave.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
The first take's chords, packed close under the tune.

<Editor exercise="open-the-chords" />

<OnPass>Four chords opened, the tune on top of each. The Rhodes now reaches down past the middle of the keyboard and leaves room around the vibes.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
How high a voicing sits changes it as much as its shape.

<OnPass>Card B is the drop-2 from card A two octaves down: the gaps that ring in the middle of the keyboard blur down there. Card C spreads the chord with a 9th on top, root and 7th low and the close notes high. A rule of thumb: keep 3rds above about C3. Low notes want wide gaps; high notes can sit close.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
You can now put a chord under every note of a tune, the way a big band's saxophones play a line together; drop-2 is one of the voicings they use. Drums and bass come from the band; the tune is yours, and so are the voicings under it.
