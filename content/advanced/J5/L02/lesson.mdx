<Step id="S3">
Neither of the two chords before home belongs to C major.

<Example id="back-door" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Fm7 is the sad iv borrowed from C minor, now with its 7th, and B♭7 is the dominant built on ♭7. Its 7th, A♭, falls a half step to G, while its root climbs a whole step to C.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
The same way home, now in G.

<Editor exercise="back-door-in-g" />

<OnPass>Cm7, F7, Gmaj7: the bass climbs from F to G, and the E♭ that colours both new chords falls a half step to D. Coming home from the dominant on ♭7, after the minor iv, is the **backdoor progression**: softer than D7 to G, and a little wistful.</OnPass>
</Step>
