<Step id="S1">
Ghost notes taught you to play some hits quieter than others. The rhythm track starts there, inside one bar: which of its sixteen places are heavy, and which are light?

<Example id="shaped" views="code,roll" autoplay />

<OnPass>In the second loop the hits on the beats ring out and the hits between them sink back. The hi-hat alone now tells you where each beat falls, the way a drummer's hand does.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Your hands can find the heavy places before you count a single one.

<Tap exercise="tap-one-and-three" />

<OnPass>Beats 1 and 3: the two heaviest places of a 4/4 bar. Beat 1 is the heavier of the two.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Your taps found two heavy places. The velocity string ranks all sixteen.

<Example id="the-levels" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Five levels, heaviest first: beat 1, then beat 3, then beats 2 and 4, then the four “ands”, then the eight sixteenths between them. That ranking is **metric weight**: what a listener expects of each place in the bar, whether or not anyone plays it louder.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Syncopation plays against that ranking.

<Predict exercise="leave-three-empty" />
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
A note pulls against the beat when it sits on a light place and the heavier place after it stays silent. Here is the groove before the move.

<Editor exercise="pull-against-three" />

<OnPass>One note, one sixteenth early, and beat 3 left to the listener: the bass now leans into the middle of the bar. The push works only because beat 3 is empty. Your ear still expects weight there, and the silence makes you feel it.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
Not every pull is equally strong. Weigh four pairs by ear.

<Choice exercise="which-pulls-harder" />

<OnPass>The hardest pull is a note on a light place in front of an empty heavy one. The last sixteenth of a bar in front of a missing downbeat pulls hardest of all. A note on an off-beat with the beat after it played does not pull at all.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
A sixteenth used to be just a sixteenth. Now you can rank the sixteen places of a bar and choose which heavy one to leave empty. While the bass pulls, give the hi-hat its whispers back with a velocity string like the one you read.

<Make exercise="your-bass-pulls" />
